# Lights Out Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 28, 2026_

Lights Out blocks the apps you choose while your iPhone is in your bedroom during the hours you set. It's built so that nothing about you leaves your phone.

## The short version

- Lights Out has no account, no servers, no analytics and no ads.
- Everything it stores stays on your device.
- It doesn't collect, sell or share any personal data, and it doesn't track you.

## What Lights Out uses, and why

**Screen Time (Family Controls).** You pick which apps, categories and websites to block with Apple's own picker. Apple gives Lights Out opaque tokens for your choices, not the names of the apps, and Lights Out never sees how you use them. The tokens are stored on your device so the block can be applied at bedtime.

**Location.** Lights Out uses iOS beacon monitoring to notice when your iPhone is near your bedroom beacon. iOS reports only "near the beacon" or "not near it". Lights Out doesn't read, store or send your GPS position or any map location.

**Bluetooth.** If you use a spare iPad or iPhone instead of a beacon, Lights Out uses Bluetooth between your own devices to tell whether you're in the bedroom. Nothing is sent anywhere else.

**Settings and activity log.** Your bedtime hours, beacon ID and a short log of recent events (such as "Entered bedroom") are stored on your device, in storage shared only between Lights Out and its own extensions. The log keeps the last 60 entries.

## What leaves your device

Nothing. Lights Out makes no network requests. If you've turned on sharing with app developers in iOS Settings, Apple may send crash reports to the developer. Those are covered by Apple's privacy policy, not handled by Lights Out.

## Deleting your data

Deleting Lights Out removes everything it stored. To lift all blocks without deleting it, go to Settings → Screen Time → Apps with Screen Time Access and turn Lights Out off.

## Children

Lights Out is for adults managing their own phone. It doesn't knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
