Install the bedroom hub

A one-time step on a Mac or PC that takes about a minute. After this, the hub only needs a USB charger by your bed.

You need: a Seeed XIAO ESP32-C6 (see Choose a beacon), a USB-C cable that carries data (some only charge), and Chrome or Edge on a Mac or PC. An iPhone or iPad can’t do this step, and neither can Safari or Firefox.

Steps

  1. Plug the XIAO into your computer with the USB-C cable.
  2. Click Install. Chrome shows your board in a small box: click it, then Connect.
  3. Wait about a minute until it says it’s installed. Then unplug it.

Installed. Now back to your iPhone. Unplug the board and plug it into a charger by your bed. Goodnight Phone finds it on its own and shows Found your board: tap Use this board, then stand in the bedroom doorway and tap Set boundary here.

Your board didn’t show up? Try another USB-C cable first: many only charge. If it still isn’t listed, put the board in install mode: hold the tiny button marked B (next to the USB-C port), tap the one marked R, then let go of B.

The board didn’t respond. Put it in install mode, then try again: hold the tiny button marked B (next to the USB-C port), tap the one marked R, then let go of B.

The hub software didn’t download. Check your internet connection, then try again.

This board isn’t a XIAO ESP32-C6. The hub only installs on that board. Check the name printed on it, or see Choose a beacon.

The install stopped partway. Plug the board straight into the computer (not through a hub), keep it plugged in, and try again. A different cable can help too.

Something else is using the board. Close any other app or browser tab that’s connected to it, then try again.

This browser can’t install the hub. Open this page in Chrome or Edge on a Mac or PC.

This page needs a secure connection. Open it at https://goodnightphone.marks-labs.com/hub/install.

Then, by the bed

  1. Plug the hub, on its USB-C adapter, into a side USB-C port of the outlet cube (or any USB-C charger) near your bed, ideally on the far side from the door. In a side port it lies flat along the wall.
  2. In Goodnight Phone, go back to the Seeed XIAO board screen (Add beacon… → Seeed XIAO board). It finds the board on its own and shows Found your board: tap Use this board.
  3. Stand in the bedroom doorway and tap Set boundary here. Everyone in the house does this on their own phone; one hub works for up to three phones, each with its own bedtime.

The hub has no Wi-Fi and no account. It gets the time from your phone over Bluetooth, so after a power cut it picks up again as soon as your phone reconnects.
