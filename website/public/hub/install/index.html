Install the bedroom hub

A one-time step on a Mac or PC that takes about a minute. After this, the hub only needs a USB charger by your bed.

You need: a Seeed XIAO ESP32-C6 (see Choose a beacon), a USB-C cable that carries data (some only charge), and Chrome or Edge on a Mac or PC. An iPhone or iPad can’t do this step, and neither can Safari or Firefox.

Steps

  1. Plug the XIAO into your computer with the USB-C cable.
  2. Click Install below, pick the board in the list that pops up (it’s usually called “USB JTAG/serial debug unit”), then confirm.
  3. Wait until it says the installation is complete, about a minute. Then unplug it.

This browser can’t install the hub. Open this page in Chrome or Edge on a Mac or PC. This page needs a secure connection. Open it at https://goodnightphone.marks-labs.com/hub/install.

The board doesn’t show up in the list

Try another cable first: charge-only cables are common. If it still doesn’t appear, unplug the board, hold the small B (boot) button, plug it back in, let go, and click Install again.

Then, by the bed

  1. Plug the hub into a USB charger or a power strip’s USB-C port near your bed, ideally on the far side from the door.
  2. In Goodnight Phone, open Bedroom beacon → Change beacon… → Use a bedroom hub. Your iPhone finds the hub on its own.
  3. Stand in the bedroom doorway and tap Set boundary here. Everyone in the house does this on their own phone; one hub works for up to three phones, each with its own bedtime.

The hub has no Wi-Fi and no account. It gets the time from your phone over Bluetooth, so after a power cut it picks up again as soon as your phone reconnects.
